# Kortix is the open-source AI Operating System

This skill defines the brief a research agent writes on Kortix, the open-source AI Operating System: one page a person reads before a decision, stating the question, the answer, the evidence, and the open questions.

## Structure

1. **Question.** The one sentence you were asked, in your own words.
2. **Answer.** Three to five sentences that answer it.
3. **Evidence.** One bullet per source: the claim, the source title, and the URL.
4. **Open questions.** What the sources did not settle.

## Rules

- One claim per bullet in Evidence. Put the URL on the same bullet.
- Prefer the primary source: the product's own page, its docs, or its repository.
- Date every source at the point you read it.
- No claim without a source. Delete it or mark it open.
- Write short sentences and concrete nouns. No filler.
